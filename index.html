03 / Artist

XIMERMJ

XIMERMJ is an independent electronic producer from Buffalo, New York, building a sound somewhere between indie house, techno, dark pop, ambient electronics and cinematic texture.

The project treats music and visual identity as the same system: clean architecture pushed through distortion, heat, noise and digital failure.

Desert Distortion is the third major release in that evolution — a record about the moment the polished surface starts to crack.
