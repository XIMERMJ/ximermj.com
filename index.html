01 / Artist

The fantasy
collapses.

XIMERMJ is the electronic project of John Richner, an independent producer working between glitch-driven house, techno, dark pop and cinematic electronic music.

The project combines warped production with a visual language built around damaged color, digital decay, industrial atmosphere and surreal architecture.

In 2026, XIMERMJ enters a new era through Afterburn and the forthcoming third EP, Desert Distortion — a world of empty pools, desert heat, mid-century architecture and Palm Springs modernism after the shine wears off.
