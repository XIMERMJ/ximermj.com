02 XIMERMJ

Built independently.
Designed as a world.

XIMERMJ is an independent electronic artist, producer and composer from the Buffalo, New York area.

The project moves between experimental dance, dark atmosphere and conceptual art, placing texture and sonic storytelling ahead of conventional song structure.

Music, visual direction and catalog development are handled independently through Richner Records LLC.
Positioning

Instrumental electronic music shaped by atmosphere, experimentation and a deliberately nontraditional sense of structure.
