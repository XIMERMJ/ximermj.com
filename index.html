The world of Afterburn

“Afterburn” is the start of a new XIMERMJ era built around Palm Springs modernism after the shine wears off — empty pools, desert heat, mid-century architecture, damaged color, and digital decay.

The track has a tense, warped electronic feel and sets up the darker visual world of the next EP, planned for late October or early November 2026.
