About XIMERMJ

XIMERMJ is an independent electronic music producer based in Buffalo, New York. The project moves between indie house, techno, dark pop, ambient and cinematic electronic music.

Built around atmosphere and contrast, the sound explores digital chaos, surrealism, movement and emotional tension. Each release develops a distinct visual and sonic world rather than following a fixed formula.

Operating independently through Richner Records LLC, XIMERMJ continues to build an evolving catalog and creative identity outside the traditional label system.
