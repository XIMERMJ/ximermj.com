01 / Artist
XIMERMJ is the independent electronic project of John Richner, based in Buffalo, New York.
XIMERMJ operates somewhere between electronic production, glitch aesthetics, house, techno, dark pop and cinematic sound design.

The project focuses on atmosphere, digital distortion and emotional tension rather than fitting into a single genre.

Releases are issued through Richner Records LLC.

EP3 / November 13, 2026
Desert Distortion is the third XIMERMJ EP and the next chapter of the project.

The era is built around Palm Springs modernism, desert heat, empty pools, damaged color, mid-century architecture and digital decay.
