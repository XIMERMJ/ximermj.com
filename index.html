After the Shine Wears Off

This is not the pastel version of Palm Springs. It is the version left behind after the fantasy overheats: empty architecture, hotel light, dry pavement, distorted palms, and a landscape that feels glamorous and hostile at the same time.

Everything should still be beautiful enough that you understand why people believed the fantasy.
