EVERYTHING LOOKED PERFECT UNTIL THE IMAGE STARTED TO FAIL.

Mid-century houses, empty pools, palms and desert heat. DESERT DISTORTION imagines Palm Springs after the shine wears off — scorched, abandoned, chemically altered and digitally corrupted.

The architecture remains. The color remains. Enough of the fantasy survives to understand why anyone wanted it in the first place.
