THE FANTASY WASN'T SUPPOSED TO SURVIVE THE HEAT.

Mid-century houses, empty pools, palms and desert heat.

DESERT DISTORTION imagines Palm Springs after the shine wears off — scorched, abandoned, chemically altered and digitally corrupted.

The architecture remains. The color remains. Enough of the fantasy survives to understand why anyone wanted to stay.
“everything should still be beautiful enough that you understand why people stayed.”

DESERT DISTORTION is the third extended play from XIMERMJ.

Built around a distorted interpretation of Palm Springs, the record moves through desert heat, damaged architecture, inverted color and the remains of mid-century optimism.

Released independently through Richner Records.
