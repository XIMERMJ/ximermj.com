For press and booking

XIMERMJ (John Richner) is an instrumental electronic artist from Buffalo, NY, working in a glitch style and releasing through his own label, Richner Records LLC. His release AFTERBURN came out on October 6, 2026. It opens with a serene, floating, celestial synth that hides harmonic dissonance and dark electronic decay, and it serves as a prelude to a larger upcoming EP. In Fall 2026 he was selected for the GRAMMY Museum's Industry Sessions: Music Production 101.
