About

XIMERMJ

XIMERMJ is an independent electronic producer from Buffalo, New York, blending indie house, techno, dark pop, ambient textures, and cinematic sound design. His work explores digital chaos, surrealism, fractured signals, and the spaces between technology and emotion.
