Independent momentum

Based in Buffalo, NY, XIMERMJ is an entirely independent, self-managed electronic artist whose releases have repeatedly appeared on the U.S. iTunes Dance Albums chart, including a No. 1 peak for the 2026 EP Signature Search.

That commercial momentum is supported by a growing international audience across the United States, Brazil, the United Kingdom and beyond. Tracks including “Headway” and “Mute” reflect a catalog built through atmosphere, experimentation and forward movement.

About

XIMERMJ is the independent electronic project of producer and composer John Richner. The work moves between experimental dance, dark atmosphere and conceptual art—placing texture and sonic storytelling ahead of traditional structure.

Music, visuals and catalog direction are developed independently through Richner Records LLC.
