Based in Buffalo, NY, XIMERMJ is an entirely independent, self-managed electronic artist whose releases have repeatedly appeared on the U.S. iTunes Dance Albums chart, including a No. 1 peak for the 2026 EP Signature Search. That commercial momentum is supported by a growing international audience across the United States, Brazil, the United Kingdom and beyond. Tracks including “Headway” and “Mute” reflect a catalog built through atmosphere, experimentation and forward movement.
