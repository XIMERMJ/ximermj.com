01 / ARTIST
XIMERMJ

XIMERMJ is an independent electronic project from Buffalo, New York, built around distorted textures, electronic production, dark pop instincts and cinematic atmosphere.

The project moves between polished and damaged — combining club-oriented production with ambient space, digital artifacts and surreal visual worlds.

The current chapter is Desert Distortion.
