THE NEW ERA OF
INDEPENDENT SOUND

By Richner Review Staff

The landscape of independent music has entered a period of remarkable transformation. Artists are no longer defined by access to traditional industry infrastructure but by their ability to cultivate meaningful audiences, develop original creative identities, and distribute work across a global network of digital platforms. Richner Review exists to document that evolution through thoughtful journalism, artist interviews, critical reviews, and conversations surrounding modern music culture.
