“Afterburn” begins a new XIMERMJ era built around Palm Springs modernism after the shine wears off — empty pools, desert heat, mid-century architecture, damaged color and digital decay.

The visual world takes the polished fantasy of the desert and dismantles it. Familiar imagery is inverted, corrupted and pushed into something less pristine: scorched landscapes, artificial color and architecture that feels almost abandoned.

The track carries that same idea into the music: tense, warped electronic production introducing the darker world of XIMERMJ's third EP.
