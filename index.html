“Afterburn” begins a new XIMERMJ era built around Palm Springs modernism after the shine wears off — empty pools, desert heat, mid-century architecture, damaged color and digital decay.

The polished desert fantasy is dismantled. Familiar architecture is inverted, chemically altered and digitally corrupted — a version of Palm Springs that feels scorched, abandoned and slightly unreal.

The music follows the same idea: tense electronic production, warped textures and a darker visual language leading into XIMERMJ's third EP.
