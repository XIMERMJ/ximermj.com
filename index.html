03 / About

About XIMERMJ

XIMERMJ is an independent electronic music project based in Buffalo, New York. Blending glitch, indie house, techno, dark pop and cinematic textures, the project explores digital chaos, surrealism and the relationship between technology and human experience.

Operating independently under Richner Records LLC, XIMERMJ continues to develop an evolving sound through electronic production, atmosphere and experimental detail.
