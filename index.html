Richner Records LLC is an independent record label built around distinctive music, individual identity and creative control.

Based in New York, the label is developing a growing catalog while creating space for independent artists who approach music from their own direction.

We are interested in electronic, alternative, experimental, dark pop and forward-thinking music that does not need to fit neatly inside a category.
