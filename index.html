About This Mess

Yes, there is an actual company behind the headlines.

Richner Records is an independent boutique music and culture company based in Buffalo, New York. The label supports independent releases, music production, mastering, catalog administration, physical media and editorial projects.

Its flagship catalog includes XIMERMJ, with continued expansion into artist releases, interviews, cultural essays and original visual work.

The presentation may be unserious. The work is not.
